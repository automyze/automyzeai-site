Signal Clusters

The operational triggers we monitor.

Accounts enter the dispatch when verified hiring signals align. We look for live vacancies on Ashby and Greenhouse where companies struggle to secure qualified accounting leadership.

CLUSTER 01

London Scaleups Transitioning to First Head of Finance

Post-Seed and Series A ventures outgrowing external bookkeepers. Founders face severe administrative drag managing payroll, audit prep, and board reporting while actively searching for their first in-house Financial Controller.

Verifiable Signals
  • Active Ashby or Greenhouse posting for Head of Finance or Financial Controller in London
  • Mandatory requirement for ACA, ACCA, or CIMA qualification
  • Headcount between 20 and 100 with recent institutional funding
Jemstone wedge: Founder-led headhunting delivering pre-vetted accounting leaders with proven experience building statutory reporting and ERP stacks from scratch.
CLUSTER 02

Established Enterprises Expanding Core Finance Teams

Growth scaleups and consultancies expanding their finance departments to handle multi-entity consolidations, FP&A variance analysis, and complex IFRS 15 revenue recognition.

Verifiable Signals
  • Active hiring for Management Accountant, Finance Business Partner, or Finance Manager
  • Multi-currency or international subsidiary operations
  • Explicit requirement for NetSuite, Sage Intacct, or Xero migration experience
Jemstone wedge: Precision placement of commercially minded accountants who translate balance sheet data into actionable operational insights for leadership.
