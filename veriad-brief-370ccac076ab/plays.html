Everything we can run alongside the pain segments.

A weekly rhythm Hypothesis

  • Daily: job alerts and Sales Navigator alerts.
  • Weekly: ruling-digest day feeds R1, R9 and the founder post (LI10).
  • Monthly: competitor customer refresh and ad-library snapshots.

Risks

Handle with care.

Platform risk. Scraping LinkedIn and ad libraries carries terms-of-service risk. Rate-limit, and avoid account-level automation.

Defamation and tone. PV1, S10 and R1 sends reference real rulings and real ads. Use "possible" framing, never accuse, and have a person review each one.

UK and EU rules. Verify PECR and GDPR requirements for cold email and visitor identification with counsel. To verify

No customers. Every "customers like you" line would be fabricated. Use D1 and D5 first.

Next steps

Three ways to test this.

  • Run the cheapest checks. Pull the latest ASA rulings against the in-scope list, and a job search for H1-H3 at about 20 known brands. That turns estimates into tested ratings.
  • Start the design-partner list. Use the Catalog Exposure and Reviewer Backlog recipes to find five candidates.
  • Pick the first campaign. The Reviewer Backlog segment is the most scalable test.
