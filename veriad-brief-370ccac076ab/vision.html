02 · Vision

The broad vision: one operating system for in-house advertising.

The homepage describes adOS: planning, creative, campaigns, optimization, measurement and reporting in one loop, for marketing teams that are "building more capability in-house". The idea is larger than the wedge. The pain is also harder to date, so the signals are patterns instead of single events.

Brand core

What adOS claims, in its own words.

Finding
Core offeringEight modules. Strategy: "Your data, past campaigns, business context, and creative, all at your fingertips." Planning: "finds the optimal budget allocation across channels, placements, and formats." Creative: "Your team creates. Veriad tags assets, traces them to performance, and checks compliance and creative quality." Campaigns: one view across channels, handles setup and launch. Optimization: what to pause, where to shift budget, how to improve the post-click experience. Measurement: cross-channel attribution. Integrations: ad platforms, CRM, analytics and existing systems. Reporting: performance tied to business outcomes. Verified
AudienceIn-house marketing teams, with nav entries for Creative, Brand and Performance teams. "Empower your marketing team... with your people in control." The integration list (Adobe AEM, SAP, Dynamics 365, Snowflake, Databricks, Salesforce, HubSpot, Shopify, GA) points to large enterprises. Verified
Core problemA fragmented ad stack: data split across platforms, CRM and analytics; creative disconnected from performance; budget decisions without rationale; dependence on agencies; and pressure to "build more capability in-house" as "budgets come under scrutiny". Verified
Unique advantageThe homepage claims no mechanism. Candidates we can support: (1) it is channel-neutral across six platforms, which no single platform's own AI can be; (2) it closes the loop from creative asset to compliance to performance to budget; (3) enterprise security posture (CASA Tier 2, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 in progress, UK GDPR). Verified
Data differentiationThe hard problem is joining first-party enterprise data (CRM, warehouse, ERP revenue) with ad-platform data and asset-level creative metadata. A platform-native tool sees only its own walled garden; an agency sees only what the client shares. Veriad does not yet show how it does this. Hypothesis

Gaps on the page. There is no before-and-after pain framing, no FAQ, no pricing and no customers. Meta Advantage+ and Google Performance Max, the platform-native tools most likely to compete with adOS, are not mentioned at all. Verified

Tension scenarios

Seven moments of pressure.

Scenario 1

Agency handover

Trigger. The brand exits an agency, calls a review, or announces in-housing.

Tension. Planning, buying and reporting capability must exist in weeks, not hires-and-quarters.

Fit. adOS replaces the agency's planning, optimization and reporting stack, with "your people in control".

Scenario 2

The CFO challenges marketing ROI

Trigger. Earnings language on marketing efficiency, an ad-expense cut, or an efficiency programme.

Tension. The team must prove incrementality and shift spend with rationale.

Fit. Measurement, Planning and Reporting tied to business outcomes.

Scenario 3

Platform black box

Trigger. Heavy Advantage+ or Performance Max use, volatile results, loss of transparency.

Tension. The team cannot see what is working across channels or which creative drives it.

Fit. A channel-neutral layer that traces creative to performance.

Scenario 4

Data stack replatform

Trigger. A warehouse migration (Snowflake or Databricks), a GA4 or CRM replatform, hiring for marketing data engineers.

Tension. Ad data cannot be reconciled to business outcomes during or after the migration.

Fit. Integrations and Reporting join the warehouse, CRM and ad platforms.

Scenario 5

A new marketing leader

Trigger. A new CMO, chief growth officer or VP Marketing is appointed.

Tension. A first-100-days mandate, an inherited fragmented stack, pressure to show quick wins.

Fit. Fast time to value and a unified view.

Scenario 6

A new channel

Trigger. The brand's first ads on TikTok, Reddit, Pinterest or Snapchat, or a new region.

Tension. Skills, creative formats and measurement are not yet built for the channel.

Fit. Cross-channel planning and creative tagging.

Scenario 7

Agency upheaval

Trigger. Holding-company mergers, layoffs, client reassignment, fee renegotiation. To verify

Tension. Service quality and cost uncertainty on the agency side.

Fit. The alternative is already part of the "reduce reliance on agencies" message.

Selected for data recipes: Scenario 1 (with Scenarios 5 and 7 as timing amplifiers) and Scenario 4 (with Scenario 2 as the pressure amplifier). Scenario 4 is where the data advantage matters most.

Blueprints

Who is in the pain, and why now.

Blueprint A

The agency handover

Ideal customer. An enterprise brand (Gartner's sample is mostly $1B+ revenue Secondary) with $50M+ ad spend across 3 or more of Veriad's 6 channels, a recent agency change or new marketing leader, and active hiring for planning, paid media and marketing-ops roles. Personas. CMO, Head of In-House Agency, VP Performance. Hypothesis

Why Veriad wins now. The tension is time-boxed: the agency's planning and reporting layer disappears faster than a hiring plan can replace it. The homepage already speaks to this audience.

Where it loses: Meta and Google offer "free" automation inside their own platforms, and incumbents have case studies Veriad does not.
Blueprint B

The data stack cannot answer the CFO

Ideal customer. A large company rebuilding marketing measurement on a warehouse, under budget scrutiny, with a stack overlapping Veriad's integrations. Personas. VP Performance, Head of Marketing Operations, Head of Marketing Analytics. Hypothesis

Why Veriad wins now. This is the most defensible data story. A platform-native tool cannot join warehouse revenue to cross-channel spend, and the CFO's question ("what did marketing return?") maps to the Measurement and Reporting modules.

Where it loses: the proof is missing; the homepage incrementality chart is a sample, not customer evidence.
