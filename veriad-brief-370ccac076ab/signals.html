04 · Signal clusters

What fires a send: clusters, single signals, hiring and launches.

A signal is a dated public fact. A cluster is several signals that together prove a company is in the painful situation right now. Some signals are strong enough to send on alone; others only count once a second one lines up.

Fit labels show which door a signal serves. Source and reliability notes are estimates unless tagged Verified.

Signal velocity

Timing sits inside the recipe.

Three related signals inside three days is a company actively evaluating. The same three across three months is a company that happens to exist. Every data point is date-stamped, gaps between signals are measured, compressed clusters go to immediate outreach, and spread ones go to monitoring.

Tier 1

Triggers

  • Regulator names the brand (90 days)
  • A rule effective date inside the last 30 days
  • A compliance or review role under 60 days old
  • An agency change or new marketing leader inside 6 months
  • Three or more build-out hires in 60 days
A trigger can start a send on its own when it directly proves the pain.
Tier 2

Tension confirmers

  • Ad-library creative surge against a stored baseline
  • Gen-AI creative adoption
  • An in-housing announcement
  • A false-advertising suit
  • Review-pain voice (Glassdoor, G2, forums)
  • Budget-scrutiny language in the latest earnings cycle
Confirmers make a trigger more likely to be true and more urgent.
Tier 3

Qualifiers

  • Regulated vertical
  • $50M+ ad-spend proxy (filings, accounts)
  • UK or EU footprint
  • Stack overlap with Veriad's integrations
  • At least 3 of Veriad's 6 channels in use
Qualifiers decide whether the account is in scope, not whether it is in pain.

Named clusters

Seven combinations that prove a situation.

Cluster A

Post-ruling exposure Wedge

  • A regulator ruling or warning letter naming the brand (90 days)
  • The same claim wording still live in an ad library
  • An ad-spend scale qualifier (filings or accounts)
Timing: under 90 days; under 30 days is the strongest window.
Cluster B

Review queue strain Wedge

  • A review or compliance role open 30+ days, or reposted
  • Active creative count 2x or more against baseline
  • Review-pain voice in employee or software reviews
Timing: role under 60 days old, with a creative surge on a stored baseline.
Cluster C

Rule-date retrofit Wedge

  • A rule effective date inside the last 30 days To verify
  • Generic claims still present on live pages (crawl, then human review)
  • An EU or UK ad footprint
Bench cluster: testable by crawling, but needs human review to avoid false positives.
Cluster D

Gen-AI volume push Both

  • A gen-AI creative production announcement
  • A volume surge in ad libraries
  • No compliance or review hires posted
Timing: announcement and surge inside the same 90 days.
Cluster E

Agency handover Vision

  • An agency change, review or in-housing announcement (6 months), or a new leader (180 days)
  • Three or more planning, paid-media or marketing-ops postings in 60 days
  • A first-time ad channel in the last 90 days
Timing: leadership or agency change inside 6 months and a hiring cluster inside 60 days.
Cluster F

Spend-to-revenue strain Vision

  • Two or more marketing-data roles in 60 days (Snowflake, Databricks, MMM, incrementality)
  • "Marketing efficiency" language in the latest earnings cycle, or an ad-expense decline
  • Stack overlap with Veriad's integrations
Timing: hiring cluster inside 60 days and scrutiny language in the latest cycle.
Cluster G

Competitor-curious Both

  • Engagement on a competitor's LinkedIn posts or job ads
  • A negative review of a competitor on G2 or Capterra
  • Category search intent ("[competitor] alternative")
Timing: engagement inside 3 weeks; pairs with any pain cluster above.
