The Strategic Wedge

Why "we do Reddit marketing" fails, and where Novantro wins.

Every company on this list has already decided Reddit matters. Several postings say it outright: Redditors ignore brand accounts, and the channel only works with a native voice. A pitch that sounds like outsourced posting reads as exactly what they are hiring to avoid.

These teams have just opened a headcount to own Reddit themselves. Pitching an agency that posts for them competes with the hire they already approved, and triggers the "we want an authentic voice, not a vendor" objection their own postings spell out.

The High-Converting Wedge

Positioning as the Hire's Head Start

A new community hire starts with zero karma, zero subreddit standing, and no way to remove a damaging thread. Novantro gives that hire subreddit-native writers, an established account base, and legal removal of negative posts from week one, so the role they just opened shows results this quarter instead of after a long karma-building ramp.

Signal Clusters

Two ways a company tells you Reddit has no owner.

Accounts enter this dispatch only when the company's own hiring language names Reddit as a channel it intends to build. We pull the full posting text from each company's applicant tracking system and keep a dated copy before drafting a single word.

CLUSTER 01

Building Reddit In-House From Zero

The company has opened a first or dedicated community seat with Reddit in scope and no existing playbook. The hire will spend months earning standing in subreddits before any of it compounds.

Verifiable Signals
  • Live posting for a first community hire or a Reddit-specific role
  • Language like "from zero", "no playbook to inherit", or a subreddit not yet launched
  • Reddit listed alongside review surfaces such as Trustpilot or G2
Novantro wedge: aged, high-karma accounts and subreddit-native writers give the new hire reach on day one instead of after a long karma-building ramp.
CLUSTER 02

Reddit Named, But Owned Part-Time

Reddit appears as one line inside a broad growth or community role that also owns SEO, content, outbound, or five other platforms. The intent is real; the hours to do Reddit properly are not.

Verifiable Signals
  • Reddit named inside a generalist growth, organic, or community job description
  • Same role also owns AI-search or answer-engine visibility
  • Small team where this hire is one of very few open roles
Novantro wedge: run Reddit as a managed, reported program (Slack and Sheets dashboards) so the generalist hire keeps the credit without spending the hours.
