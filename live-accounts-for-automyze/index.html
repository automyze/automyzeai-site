The Strategic Wedge

Why hiring 5 manual SDRs fails, and where evidence-backed outbound wins.

When growing B2B SaaS companies hit post-PMF scale, the default playbook is hiring 3 to 5 SDRs armed with Apollo and basic sequencing tools. Within 90 days, domain deliverability crashes, prospects receive generic cold blasts, and customer acquisition costs spike. Automyze builds managed outbound infrastructure working backwards from closed deals, uncovering public evidence that catches ready buyers early.

The Commodity Trap

Manual SDR Apollo Blasting

SDRs pull broad firmographic lists (50-200 employees, VP title) and blast templated emails. Domains burn, unsubscribe rates skyrocket, and response rates drop below 0.8%.

The High-Converting Wedge

Automyze identifies public web breadcrumbs (specific ATS requisitions, tech stack migrations, regulatory filings) and handcrafts non-transferable value playbooks with live account proof before asking for a call.
