Signal Clusters

The two triggers we verified in full.

Both read from the original regulator text, not summarized from a secondary source, before either was used in a message.

CLUSTER 01

RBI's New Cyber Clock for NBFCs

RBI's (NBFC, Cybersecurity, Technology Risk, Resilience and Assurance Framework) Directions, 2026 took effect July 31, 2026, with no transition period. Middle Layer and above NBFCs now report cyber incidents within six hours, through a platform called DAKSH.

Verifiable Signals
  • RBI/DoS/2026-27/461, confirmed directly at RBI's own Master Directions page
  • Six hour DAKSH incident reporting plus proactive CERT-In notification for Middle Layer and above
  • Replaces RBI's older NBFC IT-governance framework outright, roughly seven weeks old as of today
Seconize wedge: a live compliance gap right now, not a future deadline, since there was no staggered transition period.
CLUSTER 02

The Granular Surface Inside SEBI's CSCRF

Under the ISO 27001 headline, CSCRF hides a 23 parameter scored index with a public Fail band, a Software Bill of Materials mandate for every regulated entity, and a small entity Market SOC deadline nearly two years old.

Verifiable Signals
  • ISO 27001 mandatory for Qualified REs and MIIs since August 20, 2025, checkable via IAF CertSearch
  • Cyber Capability Index: 23 weighted parameters, one sub-domain miss drops the whole score to Fail
  • SBOM requirement (standard GV.SC.S5) applies to every RE, deadline already passed February 2025
Seconize wedge: the fine print most compliance content never mentions, verified against the 205 page circular itself.
SUBJECT: YOUR SIX HOURSGrounded, not simulated

RBI's new NBFC cybersecurity directions, effective July 31, now require Middle Layer and above NBFCs to report cyber incidents on the DAKSH platform within six hours of detection, replacing the older IT-governance framework entirely.

That is a tighter clock than most existing incident-response runbooks were built around, and DAKSH itself is new enough that some teams have not wired it into the actual process yet, only into the policy document.
