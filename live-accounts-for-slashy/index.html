Signal Clusters

The operational triggers we monitor.

Accounts enter the dispatch when multiple observable facts align simultaneously. We monitor live hiring requisitions and CRM infrastructure changes in primary records before drafting a single word.

CLUSTER 01

Founder-Led Sales Transitions

Startups moving from founder-led demos to their first sales hires. Founders lose up to 15 hours weekly answering buyer threads, logging calls, and updating deal stages manually while recruiting their first Founding AE.

Verifiable Signals
  • Active Ashby or Greenhouse posting for Founding Account Executive or Head of Sales
  • Explicit requirement for HubSpot CRM pipeline hygiene and deal tracking
  • Current company headcount under 25 with founders actively running demos
Slashy wedge: Active email operator that auto-drafts responses in the founder's voice and automates CRM updates directly from Gmail, preventing pipeline leakage during the sales transition.
CLUSTER 02

High-Velocity Technical Inbound Scale

Developer platforms and AI infrastructure scaleups experiencing surges in buyer inquiries where founders struggle to separate high-ARR enterprise prospects from self-serve users.

Verifiable Signals
  • Rapid customer acquisition with 10k+ monthly active users or GitHub traction
  • Active hiring for Enterprise Account Executive on Ashby or Greenhouse
  • Documented enterprise tier launch requiring custom security and SLA reviews
Slashy wedge: Automatically surfaces high-priority enterprise conversations, generates account briefing memos, and stages replies with relevant collateral instantly.
